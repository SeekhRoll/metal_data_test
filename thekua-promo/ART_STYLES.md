# Art styles for future concepts

Each concept should feel like traditional, smooth 2D animation in a real painting or print tradition, not vector Flash animation. The "how" column says how the look can be produced with the pipeline in this repo:

- **Brush-stroke painter:** the WebGL renderer from `04-van-gogh-dussehra`, where thousands of textured strokes are anchored in the world and oriented by a direction map.
- **Layered cut-outs:** flat shapes on textured paper, moved with parallax and eased camera moves.
- **Line + wash:** ink outlines with watercolour-style fills and paper bleed.

| # | Style | What makes it recognisable | Great fit for | How |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Van Gogh (post-impressionist)** ✅ done in 04 | Thick impasto, swirling skies, halos around lights, complementary blue and orange | Night festivals: Dussehra, Diwali, Chhath evening | Brush-stroke painter |
| 2 | **Madhubani / Mithila** | Double ink outlines, every space filled with patterns, fish, peacocks, sun faces, natural reds, yellows and indigos | **Chhath and thekua's own home, Mithila in Bihar**. The most authentic choice for the brand | Line + wash, patterns drawn on progressively |
| 3 | **Monet (impressionist)** | Soft broken dabs, shimmering light on water, pastel mornings | Chhath sunrise at the ghat, water reflections | Brush-stroke painter with short, round dabs and no contour-following |
| 4 | **Studio Ghibli-style watercolour backgrounds** | Painterly skies, lush greens, soft cel-shaded characters, gentle wind in grass | Nostalgic family and childhood stories | Line + wash backgrounds, cel characters, parallax |
| 5 | **Ukiyo-e woodblock (Hokusai / Hiroshige)** | Flat colour planes, Prussian blue, stylised waves and clouds, wood-grain print texture | Monsoon or river stories, bold posters | Layered cut-outs with a woodgrain overlay |
| 6 | **Mughal / Rajput / Pahari miniature** | Jewel colours, gold borders, flat perspective, fine detail, ornate frames | Royal "festive gifting" feel, Diwali hampers | Layered cut-outs inside an ornate frame, gold leaf shimmer |
| 7 | **Pichwai (Nathdwara)** | Cows, lotus ponds, dense repeating motifs on deep indigo or green, gold | Janmashtami or Govardhan, devotional mood | Layered cut-outs, pattern growth animation |
| 8 | **Kalighat pat** | Bold sweeping brush outlines, flat washes, witty everyday scenes | Humorous Delhi-life vignettes (chai, metro, neighbours) | Line + wash with calligraphic brush lines |
| 9 | **Warli** | White stick figures on terracotta, circles of dancers, rhythmic repetition | Community and togetherness, harvest | Procedural figures dancing in rings |
| 10 | **Gond** | Creatures filled with dots and dashes, vivid colours, trees of life | Nature and harvest, playful storytelling | Line + wash with pattern fills that grow |
| 11 | **Raja Ravi Varma (oil realism)** | Classical oil portraiture, rich drapery, stage-like lighting | Premium or heritage brand film | Brush-stroke painter with fine strokes and soft blending |
| 12 | **Tanjore painting** | Gold leaf relief, gemstone colours, deep reds and greens | Diwali gifting box launch | Cut-outs with embossed gold and glints |
| 13 | **Seurat pointillism** | Everything made of coloured dots, optical mixing | Short, striking social posts | Brush-stroke painter with round dot stamps |
| 14 | **Klimt gold** | Gold mosaic patterns, spirals, intimate figures | Luxury or gift-pack edition | Cut-outs with gold mosaic fills |
| 15 | **Paper cut-out silhouettes (Lotte Reiniger)** | Black silhouette puppets on glowing coloured backdrops | Ramlila or Ramayana storytelling | Layered cut-outs, jointed puppets |
| 16 | **Chinese / Japanese ink wash (sumi-e)** | Expressive single-brush strokes, lots of empty space, soft bleeds | Calm, minimal "homemade with care" | Line + wash with bleeding strokes |
| 17 | **Stained glass** | Leaded black lines, luminous jewel tones, light pouring through | Festive nights, temples | Cut-outs with a glow pass |
| 18 | **Chalk pastel / crayon on kraft paper** | Grainy strokes, visible paper tooth | Kids and family, school-tiffin stories | Brush-stroke painter with a grainy stamp |
| 19 | **Charcoal rotoscope (Loving Vincent-like realism)** | Sketchy tonal drawing, smudges, moving light | Emotional, cinematic storytelling | Brush-stroke painter in grayscale with smudge |
| 20 | **Claymation / stop-motion look** | Soft plasticine forms, thumbprints, 12 fps charm | Playful product demos | Cut-outs with a clay texture and stepped timing |
