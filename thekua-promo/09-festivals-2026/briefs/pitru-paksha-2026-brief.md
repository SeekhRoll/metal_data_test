# Pitru Paksha 2026: Trilogy in Three Traditional Art Forms

**Period:** Pitru Paksha, 27 September to 10 October 2026 (Mahalaya Amavasya on 10 October)
**Episodes:** 3 reverential story videos, about 90–100s each
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 24fps
**Language:** Hindi VO with burned-in Devanagari subtitles

| Episode | Story | Art form | Region | Live by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | भगीरथ प्रयास | **पहाड़ी लघुचित्र** (Pahari miniature, Kangra) | Himachal Pradesh | 2 Oct |
| 2 | इंदिरा एकादशी | **पट्टचित्र** (Pattachitra) | Odisha | 5–6 Oct (**date-bound**) |
| 3 | फल्गु के तट पर | **मंजूषा कला** (Manjusha) | Bhagalpur, Bihar | 8–9 Oct |

**Why these forms:**
- **Pahari:** a Himalayan story, and the descent of the Ganga is a classic Pahari subject. It also shares the style system being built for the Navratri Format B and C videos.
- **Pattachitra:** a deeply Vaishnav tradition, well suited to Vishnu, Vaikuntha and Narada.
- **Manjusha:** a Bihar story told in a Bihar art form.

---

## 1. Series rules (Pitru Paksha tone)

1. **Reverential, not celebratory:** no festive music, no bright party visuals, no "offer" or "sale" language.
2. **No product menu, no prices, no phone number.** The only brand presence is the closing sign-off (section 4).
3. **Stories are about ancestors, remembrance and duty.** No link to the products.
4. **Sensitive moments** (death, curses, the realm of Yama) are handled with restraint: light, silhouette and stillness. Nothing frightening or graphic.
5. **Every opening title names the art form** and its region (section 3).
6. **Excluded styles:** no Mughal, Deccani or Persian-influenced court styles, motifs or architecture.

---

## 2. Reverential treatment (applies to all three styles)

| Element | Setting |
|---|---|
| Palette | Each style's traditional palette, **muted by about 15%** in saturation, with warmer, dimmer overall light |
| Pacing | Slow. Longer holds, and pauses between VO lines |
| Transitions | Fade to near-dark, then return. No energetic wipes |
| Atmosphere | A faint incense-smoke haze drifting upward (low-opacity noise layer) |
| Music | Tanpura drone with soft bansuri or sarangi. **No percussion** |
| Divine moments | Slow golden bloom |

## 4. Closing sign-off (all episodes, about 5s)

- **Visual:** the scene fades to near-dark, leaving a single brass diya with a steady flame at the centre, **drawn in that episode's art form**.
- **Text (two lines, in a reserved text zone):**
  - पितरों को नमन।
  - Shri Desi Thekua की ओर से
- **Audio:** tanpura only, with no VO (or an optional soft VO: "पितरों को नमन।").
- **Nothing else:** no products, prices or contact details.

## 5. Style specifications

All styles use the standard stack (section 9) and the standard motion principles: replacement poses on twos, eased timing, no rigid limb rotation, and gentle idle life.

### 5.1 Pahari miniature (Episode 1)
- **Look:**
  - Flat opaque colour, fine even outlines, and a wide red outer border with a thin floral inner margin.
  - Rolling hills, rounded trees with individually drawn leaves, and a band of sky.
  - Graceful figures with long eyes and fine jewellery.
  - Gold on crowns and ornaments, and haloes behind divine figures.
  - Rajput-Pahari architecture only (no Mughal arches or domes).
- **Motion:**
  - A fine-line reveal, then flat colour fills in, then the gold shimmer passes.
  - Slow landscape pans with 3-layer parallax (foreground trees, figures, far hills).
- **Shared system:** the same Pahari style system as the Navratri plan (section 3 there), with the muted reverential palette applied.

### 5.2 Pattachitra, Odisha (Episode 2)
- **Look:**
  - Red or ochre backgrounds with intricate multi-band floral borders.
  - Figures with elongated eyes and sharp profiles, heavily ornamented.
  - Dense, stylised foliage and fine line detail throughout.
- **Signature motion:**
  - Border bands draw in one after another at the start of each scene.
  - Ornaments catch a soft highlight.
- **Realms:**
  - The dim realm is a darker, cooler-toned panel inside the same border system.
  - Vaikuntha appears as a radiant golden panel opening above it.

### 5.3 Manjusha, Bhagalpur (Episode 3)
- **Look:**
  - Strictly three colours (pink, green and yellow) with bold black outlines on a light ground.
  - Scenes sit inside box-like bordered compartments ("manjusha" means box).
  - Borders use traditional bands, especially the **lahariya (wave)** border for the Phalgu.
- **Signature motion:**
  - Compartments open like the panels of a box.
  - The wave border flows continuously.
  - The river sinking beneath the sand is shown by the wave band sliding under a sand-textured band.
- **The Akshayavat:** drawn as a large, symmetrical, bordered tree.

## 6. Episode 1: भगीरथ प्रयास (Bhagiratha's Effort) · Pahari miniature · about 90s

**Source:** Ramayana (Bala Kanda), and the Puranas.
**Moral:** some duties span generations; persistence can bring even the Ganga down to earth.

1. **0–6s · Title**
   - **Visual:** A painted album page opens. Title card: भगीरथ प्रयास / पहाड़ी लघुचित्र शैली में · हिमाचल प्रदेश / पितृ पक्ष विशेष.
   - **VO:** पितृ पक्ष में, एक कथा — पहाड़ी लघुचित्र शैली में। अपने पुरखों के लिए किए गए, सबसे बड़े प्रयास की।
2. **6–18s · King Sagar's horse**
   - **Visual:** King Sagar's yagya in a hill-palace courtyard. The sacrificial horse vanishes, and the shadow of Indra leads it away underground.
   - **VO:** राजा सगर ने अश्वमेध यज्ञ किया। पर यज्ञ का घोड़ा चुरा लिया गया, और पाताल में छिपा दिया गया।
3. **18–30s · The 60,000 sons**
   - **Visual:** A long line of identical sons digs downward and finds the horse beside sage Kapila, who is deep in meditation. The sons point accusingly.
   - **VO:** सगर के साठ हज़ार पुत्र उसे खोजते हुए, धरती खोदकर पाताल पहुँचे। घोड़ा, महर्षि कपिल के पास बँधा था। उन्होंने ऋषि पर ही चोरी का आरोप लगा दिया।
4. **30–38s · Kapila's gaze (restrained)**
   - **Visual:** Kapila opens his eyes. A wave of white light passes over the sons, and they fade to pale silhouettes that dissolve into drifting motes. Silence.
   - **VO:** ऋषि का ध्यान टूटा… और उनकी दृष्टि से, सब भस्म हो गए।
5. **38–50s · Generations of effort**
   - **Visual:** Three generations of kings in sequence (Anshuman, Dilip, Bhagiratha), each fading into the next.
   - **VO:** उन्हें मुक्ति केवल गंगा के जल से मिल सकती थी। पीढ़ियाँ बीत गईं… कोई सफल न हुआ। फिर आए भगीरथ।
6. **50–60s · Tapasya**
   - **Visual:** Bhagiratha stands on one foot among Himalayan peaks as seasons cycle behind him. Brahma appears and grants the boon.
   - **VO:** भगीरथ ने वर्षों कठोर तप किया। ब्रह्मा प्रसन्न हुए — गंगा धरती पर आएँगी।
7. **60–72s · Shiva catches the Ganga**
   - **Visual:** The Ganga (the goddess on a makara, trailing painted water lines) crashes down from the sky. Shiva spreads his jata and catches the whole flood. The river then emerges as a single gentle stream.
   - **VO:** पर गंगा का वेग धरती सह न पाती। तब शिव ने उन्हें अपनी जटाओं में थाम लिया, और धीरे से एक धारा छोड़ी।
8. **72–82s · Liberation**
   - **Visual:** Bhagiratha leads the river in his chariot. The water flows over the ashes, and 60,000 small points of light rise upward.
   - **VO:** भगीरथ के पीछे-पीछे, गंगा चलीं… और जैसे ही उनका जल उन भस्मों पर पड़ा, साठ हज़ार पुरखों को मुक्ति मिल गई।
9. **82–88s · Moral**
   - **Visual:** Bhagiratha alone at the riverbank, with his hands folded.
   - **Text and VO:** आज भी बड़े प्रयास को, "भगीरथ प्रयास" कहते हैं।
10. **88–93s · Closing sign-off** (section 4).

## 7. Episode 2: इंदिरा एकादशी (Indira Ekadashi) · Pattachitra · about 90s

**Source:** the Indira Ekadashi katha, as Krishna tells it to Yudhishthira in Puranic tradition.
**Moral:** the good we do can reach those we have lost.
**Timing:** Indira Ekadashi falls within Pitru Paksha (around 7 October; confirm with the Delhi panchang). **Post on 5 or 6 October.**

1. **0–6s · Title**
   - **Visual:** Border bands draw in. Title card: इंदिरा एकादशी / पट्टचित्र शैली में · ओडिशा / पितृ पक्ष विशेष.
   - **VO:** पितृ पक्ष की एकादशी की कथा — ओडिशा की पट्टचित्र शैली में।
2. **6–18s · King Indrasen**
   - **Visual:** King Indrasen of Mahishmati on his throne, a just and devout ruler, with happy subjects.
   - **VO:** माहिष्मती के राजा इंद्रसेन, धर्मात्मा और प्रजा-प्रिय थे।
3. **18–30s · Narada arrives**
   - **Visual:** Narada descends, playing his veena. The king welcomes him.
   - **VO:** एक दिन देवर्षि नारद उनके दरबार में आए, और बोले — राजन, मैं यमलोक से आ रहा हूँ।
4. **30–44s · The father's message (restrained)**
   - **Visual:** A dim, cool-toned panel inside the border. The king's father sits quietly, not suffering, just waiting, and speaks gently. No depiction of punishment.
   - **VO:** वहाँ मैंने आपके पिता को देखा। उन्होंने संदेश भेजा है — "एक बार मुझसे एकादशी का व्रत टूट गया था। मेरा पुत्र, आश्विन मास की इंदिरा एकादशी का व्रत करे, और उसका पुण्य मुझे दे।"
5. **44–58s · The vow**
   - **Visual:** Indrasen and his family perform shradh by the river, fast through the day in prayer, and on the next day feed guests and give daan.
   - **VO:** राजा ने पूरे परिवार के साथ विधि से व्रत किया — पितरों का श्राद्ध, दिन भर उपवास, और अगले दिन दान।
6. **58–72s · The father's release**
   - **Visual:** The dim panel brightens. A golden Vaikuntha panel opens above it, and a celestial vimana descends. The father rises into golden light, and flowers fall across the scene.
   - **VO:** उसी क्षण, उनके पिता को मुक्ति मिली, और वे दिव्य विमान में, वैकुंठ की ओर चले गए।
7. **72–85s · Moral**
   - **Visual:** Indrasen places a small lamp on the river, and it floats away.
   - **Text and VO:** जो चले गए, वो दूर नहीं होते… हमारे अच्छे कर्म, उन तक पहुँचते हैं।
8. **85–90s · Closing sign-off** (section 4).

## 8. Episode 3: फल्गु के तट पर (On the Banks of the Phalgu) · Manjusha · about 100s

**Source:** the regional tradition of Gaya (Bihar), also found in some Ramayana retellings. Frame it with "कहते हैं".
**Moral:** speaking the truth, even when it is hard.

1. **0–6s · Title**
   - **Visual:** A box compartment opens. Title card: फल्गु के तट पर / मंजूषा कला शैली में · भागलपुर, बिहार / पितृ पक्ष विशेष.
   - **VO:** गया जी की एक कथा — बिहार की मंजूषा कला शैली में।
2. **6–18s · Arrival at Gaya**
   - **Visual:** Ram, Lakshman and Sita arrive at the banks of the Phalgu (lahariya wave border), beside a huge banyan, the Akshayavat.
   - **VO:** कहते हैं, वनवास के समय, श्रीराम, लक्ष्मण और सीता, राजा दशरथ का श्राद्ध करने, गया पहुँचे।
3. **18–28s · Sita waits**
   - **Visual:** Ram and Lakshman leave to gather materials for the ritual. Sita waits alone as the sun moves across the panel.
   - **VO:** राम और लक्ष्मण सामग्री लेने गए। समय बीतता गया… और श्राद्ध का मुहूर्त निकलने लगा।
4. **28–40s · Dasharatha's call (gentle)**
   - **Visual:** A soft golden glow rises from the river. Dasharatha's hands, as a glowing outline, reach up out of the light.
   - **VO:** तभी फल्गु से, दशरथ जी की आवाज़ आई — "बेटी, भूख लगी है… पिंड दो।"
5. **40–54s · The sand pinda**
   - **Visual:** Sita gathers the river sand, shapes small pindas and offers them. The witnesses stand around her: the Phalgu river, a cow, and the Akshayavat. The glowing hands receive the offering and fade in peace.
   - **VO:** सामग्री कहाँ से आती? सीता ने फल्गु की बालू से ही पिंड बनाए, और अर्पित कर दिए। फल्गु नदी, एक गाय, और अक्षयवट — ये सब साक्षी बने।
6. **54–66s · Ram returns**
   - **Visual:** Ram and Lakshman return. Ram gently asks what happened. The river and the cow turn away, silent. The Akshayavat's leaves shimmer.
   - **VO:** राम लौटे। सीता ने सब बताया। पर जब साक्षियों से पूछा गया — नदी और गाय चुप रहे। केवल अक्षयवट ने सच कहा।
7. **66–78s · The truth confirmed**
   - **Visual:** Dasharatha's voice echoes from above in a soft golden glow. Ram bows to Sita.
   - **VO:** तब आकाश से दशरथ जी की वाणी गूँजी — "सीता का पिंड मुझे मिल गया।"
8. **78–90s · The legacy**
   - **Visual:**
     - The wave band slides beneath a sand-textured band, so the river's surface stays dry while water flows below.
     - The Akshayavat glows green.
   - **VO:** कहते हैं, तभी से फल्गु, बालू के नीचे बहती है… और अक्षयवट सदा हरा रहता है। आज भी गया में, बालू के पिंड और अक्षयवट की पूजा, श्राद्ध का हिस्सा है।
9. **90–95s · Moral**
   - **Text and VO:** सच बोलना कभी-कभी कठिन होता है… पर वही अमर रहता है।
10. **95–100s · Closing sign-off** (section 4).

**Scope note:** some versions include additional witnesses who are cursed. They are deliberately left out to keep the story simple and free of any group being cast negatively.

## 10. Checks and acceptance criteria

- **Text collision:** reserved `textZones` for every scene (including the art form line), with 24px padding. Render every 6th frame and check bounding boxes. The build fails on any overlap.
- **Art form title:** the art form line is present and matches the style actually rendered.
- **Style exclusion:** no Mughal, Deccani or Persian-influenced styles, motifs or architecture.
- **Tone check:** no percussion in the audio, and no product, price or contact details anywhere.
- **Restraint check:** no graphic depiction of death, ash, punishment or curses. Only light, silhouette and stillness.
- **Subtitles:** at most 2 lines and about 28 characters per line.
- **Look:** every paused frame reads as an authentic example of that episode's art form.
- **Duration:** within ±5s of each episode's target.
