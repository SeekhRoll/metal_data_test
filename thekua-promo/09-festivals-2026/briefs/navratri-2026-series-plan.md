# Navratri 2026: Series Plan and Claude Code Brief

**Sponsor:** Sri Desi Thekua and its product range (mentioned only at the end of each video)
**Festival:** Sharad Navratri, Sunday 11 October to Monday 19 October 2026 (Dussehra on 20 October)
**Output:** 9 days × 3 formats = **27 videos**. The minimum is 1 per day (Format C).
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 24fps
**Language:** Hindi, with burned-in Devanagari text and subtitles
**Scripts:** see `navratri-2026-day-wise-scripts.md`

---

## 1. Series rules

1. **The content is about the day's Devi only.** The story, moral and facts must stand on their own, with no link to the products.
2. **Products appear only in the end card,** as a sponsor mention: "Sri Desi Thekua की ओर से".
3. **Use "Sri" spelling throughout** (Hindi: श्री), as in all earlier Sri Desi Thekua videos. *(Amended 28 Sep: brand stays "Sri", not "Shri".)*
4. **Art styles:**
   - **Formats B and C** use one unified style, **Pahari miniature** (section 3), for all 9 days, so the daily greeting and explainer are instantly recognisable.
   - **Format A** stories each use a **different regional art form** (section 4), under the series name **"नवरात्रि · नौ देवियाँ, नौ कलाएँ"**. The nine days become a journey across India's art traditions.
5. **Every opening title names the art form** and its region (section 5).
6. **Excluded styles:** no Mughal, Deccani or Persian-influenced court styles, motifs or architecture.
7. **Iconography must be accurate.** Every Devi depiction, in every style, is checked against the table in section 6.2.

### 2.1 Format C: शुभकामना (12–15s)
- **0–3s:** Border and background in the day's colour accent. A diya lights up.
- **3–9s:** The Devi portrait (Pahari) is revealed, with marigold petals falling.
- **Text:** "शुभ नवरात्रि" / "[Day] · माँ [Name]" / the mantra / the art form line (small).
- **9–12s:** Short brand strip (section 7.2). No product pitch, so it stays shareable.
- **Audio:** soft shehnai or bell loop, with an optional chanted mantra.

### 2.2 Format B: आज की देवी (25–30s)
- **0–4s:** Title placard: "नवरात्रि · [Day]", the Devi's name and the art form line.
- **4–18s:** Portrait with attribute callouts appearing one by one on small placards (vahana, items in hand, mudra). Each placard follows the collision rules (section 9).
- **18–23s:** Colour of the day (swatch and meaning) and bhog.
- **23–25s:** Mantra on screen.
- **25–30s:** Full end card (section 7.1).

### 2.3 Format A: देवी कथा (55–60s)
- **0–4s:** Title (section 5).
- **4–48s:** The story in 5–6 scenes, in the day's art form.
- **48–52s:** Final Devi portrait in the day's art form, with a one-line moral.
- **52–60s:** Full end card (section 7.1).

## 3. Pahari miniature (Kangra / Guler): the Format B/C style, and Day 1 of Format A

### 3.1 Style rules
- **Flat opaque colour** (gouache look), no gradients, with fine, even brush outlines in dark sepia or black.
- **Borders:** a wide outer border (deep red, or the day's colour) and a thin inner margin with a fine floral scroll.
- **Landscape:** rolling green hills, rounded trees with individually drawn leaves, a river, and a band of sky with a soft white or gold horizon.
- **Architecture:** Rajput-Pahari pavilions and shrines (**no Mughal arches or domes**).
- **Figures:** graceful and slender, with oval faces in profile or three-quarter view, long eyes, fine jewellery, and flowing dupattas with delicate folds.
- **Gold:** used on crowns, jewellery, weapons, haloes and border details, with an animated shimmer.
- **Halo:** a round golden nimbus behind every Devi.
- **Day colour:** used as an **accent only** (the border band, the Devi's garment or backdrop cloth, flower garlands), so the palette stays coherent across all nine days.

### 3.2 Motion rules
- **Fine-line reveal:** outlines draw on with even width, then flat colour fills in, then the gold shimmer passes.
- **Replacement animation:** 3–6 poses per character, swapped on twos. No rigid limb rotation.
- **Idle life:** diya flicker, dupatta sway, falling marigold petals, blinking, and water shimmer in rivers.
- **Camera:** slow push-ins on portraits, and pans across the landscape with 3-layer parallax (foreground trees, figures, far hills).
- **Transitions:** a painted-panel slide, as if turning the pages of a miniature album.
- **Divine moments** (manifestation, blessing) get a radiant halo bloom.
- **Timing:** eased timing with slight overshoot. Nothing moves linearly.

All styles use the standard stack (section 8.1) and the standard motion principles: replacement poses on twos, eased timing, no rigid limb rotation (except shadow puppets), and idle life.

### 4.2 Day 1: Pahari miniature
Section 3, unchanged.

### 4.3 Day 2: Sanjhi
- **Look:** ivory-white cut-paper stencils with intricate lace-like perforations, laid over a soft coloured ground. Figures and scenes are defined by the negative space that has been cut away.
- **Signature motion:** coloured powder sifts through the stencil to reveal the image beneath (particle fill clipped by a stencil mask). Stencils lift, settle and shift slightly like real paper, with soft drop shadows.
- **Seasons scene:** the stencil stays fixed while the powder colour beneath it cycles through summer ochre, monsoon blue and winter white.
- **Technique:** stencils are SVG masks with generated perforation patterns (floral, vines, lattice). Backlight glow shows through the cuts.

### 4.4 Day 3: Madhubani, wedding / kohbar variant
- **Reuse:** the existing Madhubani engine (Bharni style: double outlines, hatching, no empty space, borders).
- **Add:** kohbar wedding motifs (lotus, bamboo, fish pairs, parrots, sun and moon) for the wedding scenes.
- **Tone:** the procession of ganas stays playful and comic.

### 4.5 Day 4: Kolam
- **Look:** white rice-flour lines on a dark earth-red floor. Continuous looping lines wind around a grid of dots, with strong symmetry.
- **Signature motion:** the dots appear first, then a single continuous line draws itself around them without lifting.
  - The cosmos, the sun, life on earth and the Devi herself are all drawn as continuous-line kolam figures.
  - Accent colour (the day's royal blue and a little saffron) is added only as powder fill inside finished shapes.
- **Technique:**
  - Procedural loop-kolam generation on dot grids (grid traversal and curve algorithms) for the patterns.
  - Hand-authored single-line paths for the figures.
  - Slightly uneven line thickness and powder grain, so it looks drawn by hand.

### 4.6 Day 5: Kerala mural
- **Look:**
  - Pancha-varna palette: red ochre, yellow ochre, green (dominant), white and black.
  - Figures are large, heavily ornamented and fill the frame, with big expressive eyes and thick flowing outlines.
  - Backgrounds are dense with foliage.
- **Signature motion:** a slow shimmer on ornaments, flowing garments, and dramatic, slow push-ins on faces.
- **Technique:** layered flat fills with soft tonal modelling along the edges, plus a wall-plaster texture.

### 4.7 Day 6: Bengal Patachitra (Patua scroll)
- **Look:**
  - A long vertical scroll of stacked panels, divided by floral borders.
  - Flat bright colours (yellow, red, blue, black) with bold outlines.
- **Signature motion:** the camera travels **down the scroll panel by panel**, and each panel animates briefly as it arrives.
- **Narration:** VO in a lilting, sing-song Patua storytelling cadence (pater gaan feel).
- **Non-graphic rule:** Mahishasura's defeat is shown as a single flash of light. No gore.

### 4.8 Day 7: Shadow puppetry, night variant
- **Reuse:** the existing Chhaya Katha engine (screen, lamp, perforated translucent puppets, jointed rigs, pendulum physics).
- **Night variant:** darker screen, fewer colours, and the Devi's three eyes and necklace glowing through perforations.
- **Non-graphic rule:** Raktabeej's drops are shown as dark embers, never blood.

### 4.9 Day 8: Mysore painting
- **Look:**
  - Soft, muted, luminous colours with delicate fine lines.
  - Restrained gold gesso on ornaments and arches (subtler than Tanjore).
  - Serene faces and temple-style architectural arches framing the scene.
- **Signature motion:** gentle, with soft light blooms and a slow moonlight glow building from within the figure.
- **Framing rule:** the radiance is inner light, never skin colour (see the scripts file).

### 4.10 Day 9: Tanjore painting
- **Look:**
  - Rich, vivid colours with a large, frontal central deity.
  - A rounded face and an ornate arch (prabhavali) framing the deity.
  - **Raised gold-foil relief** on ornaments and the arch, with inlaid glass gems (red, green, white).
- **Signature motion:** a slow light sweep across the gold relief, and gems catching sparkles.
- **Technique:**
  - Fake relief with SVG lighting filters (`feDiffuseLighting`, `feSpecularLighting`) on a heightmap for the gold areas.
  - Gem highlights as small animated specular points.
- **Ardhanarishvara scene:** a clean vertical line reveal down the centre, with the left half becoming the Devi and gold relief tracing the dividing line.

## 8. Claude Code build

### 8.1 Stack
- **Remotion** (React + SVG) for all formats, rendered at 1080×1920, 24fps.
- **perfect-freehand** for brush outlines (width tuned per style).
- **flubber** for occasional morphs.
- **SVG filters** for paper grain, gold shimmer, halo bloom and Tanjore relief lighting.
- **Particle system** for Sanjhi powder fills and Kolam powder grain.
- **FFmpeg** for the final mux and batch export.
- **Data-driven compositions:** `FormatC.tsx`, `FormatB.tsx` and `stories/DayN.tsx` all read `navratri-days.json` and `menu.json`.

## 9. Checks (automated; the build fails if any fails)

1. **Text collision:**
   - Every scene declares `textZones`, including the art form line. No graphic may enter them at any frame, with 24px padding.
   - Render every 6th frame and compare the bounding boxes of text and graphic nodes.
   - Report scene, frame number and element IDs.
2. **Iconography:** each Devi component, in every style, exports its attributes (arm count, vahana, held items). A test asserts they match `navratri-days.json`.
3. **Data consistency:** the day, date, weekday, colour and mantra shown on screen match `navratri-days.json`. The end card matches `menu.json`.
4. **Art form title:** the art form line is present in every opening title and matches the style actually rendered (`artForm.styleId` for A, `pahari` for B and C).
5. **Style exclusion:** no Mughal, Deccani or Persian-influenced styles, motifs or architecture.
6. **Subtitle length:** at most 2 lines and about 28 characters per line.
7. **Duration:** C is 12–15s, B is 25–30s, A is 55–60s.

## 11. Before publishing
- **Fasting audience:** all five products use atta, maida or besan, so they are **not vrat food** for people keeping strict Navratri fasts. Do not show or describe them as vrat-friendly or as bhog. The neutral end card avoids this.
- **"No Sugar" claim:** the thekua items are sweetened with gud. Check FSSAI claims rules before using "No Sugar". "No refined sugar" or "गुड़ से बना" may be safer. This is left out of `menu.json` until confirmed.
- **Dates:** the tithi can shift a day's observance locally. Cross-check the Delhi panchang before scheduling posts.
- **Sensitive scenes:** Day 1 (Sati's sacrifice) is shown abstractly as golden yogic light. Days 6–7 have no gore. Day 8 is framed as inner radiance, with no fair-skin messaging.
- **Iconography:** have someone knowledgeable review every Devi depiction, in every style, before rendering.
